# Code of conduct

dlux follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome newcomers, assume good intent, and focus on what's best
for the project and the people in it. Harassment, insults, discriminatory language and personal
attacks are not acceptable, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is
represented.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately at
**CONDUCT_CONTACT_EMAIL**. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or
reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
